# Swedish (sv) glossary — pdflookscanned.com

Market: Sweden and Swedish-speaking Finland. Standard Sweden Swedish (rikssvenska); avoid finlandisms and
Norwegian/Danish spellings. This file is binding for everyone who writes Swedish text for the site
(interface, home guide, blog chrome, article bodies).

## Address and style

- Address the reader with **du** (lowercase, never "Du"), possessives **din/ditt/dina**. Never "ni" as polite form.
- Impersonal "man" is fine in questions people search ("Hur får man en PDF att se skannad ut?"), but body text
  talks to the reader with "du".
- Short, direct sentences. Answer first. Use the active voice where Swedish allows it ("Verktyget gör om varje
  sida till en bild"), but idiomatic s-passive is fine ("filen laddas ned", "sidorna bearbetas").
- Headings: sentence case (only the first word and proper nouns capitalized). Keep questions as questions.
  Max 70 characters. No title case ("Vad Är En Skannad PDF" is wrong).
- Verb for scanning: **skanna / skannad / skannat / skanning** (the spelling Swedish OS and app UIs use).
  "scanna/scannad" and "inskannad" are real search variants: use them only in `keywords` or once in a
  "whether you search for …" sentence, never as the running spelling.
- Adjective agreement matters: *en PDF … skannad*, *ett dokument … skannat*, *PDF:er … skannade*.
  "PDF" is common gender (en PDF, PDF:en, PDF:er, PDF:erna). Abbreviations inflect with a colon: PDF:en,
  PDF:er, ZIP-filen (with a hyphen in compounds: PDF-fil, PDF-sida, ZIP-arkiv, JPG-bild, OCR-lager).
- Compounds are written together (skanningseffekt, papperston, filstorlek); use a hyphen only after
  abbreviations, numbers and product names (PDF-fil, A4-sida, Chrome-tillägg, Word-dokument, 2×-upplösning).
- Quotation marks: ”…” (the same closing-style mark on both sides). Dash in running text: spaced en dash ( – ).
- "Gratis" and "kostnadsfri" are both fine; "gratis" is what people search. "Utan registrering" / "utan konto"
  for "no signup".
- Honest-use wording: "ärligt", "officiella handlingar", "id-handlingar", "namnteckningar som inte är dina".
- Brand: **Make PDF look scanned** stays in English, unchanged, as a proper name. Refer to it as
  "verktyget", "gratisverktyget", "det kostnadsfria verktyget Make PDF look scanned". Never translate it.
  In compounds rewrite: "teamet bakom Make PDF look scanned" (not "Make PDF look scanned-teamet").
- Never name competitors or third-party apps (see TRANSLATOR-GUIDE). Say "ett bildredigeringsprogram",
  "en skannerapp i mobilen", "PDF-program för datorn". Never "Google Drive"-scanning.

## Numbers, dates, units

- Decimal comma: 0,3 · 0,5° · 1,2 · 0,05–0,2. Values stay exactly as in English.
- Thousands separator: space (non-breaking in HTML if possible: `2&nbsp;200 px`), e.g. 2 200 px, 10 000 sidor.
- Ranges with en dash, no spaces: 0,5–2°, 20–60 sekunder, 300–400 %.
- Percent: Swedish norm is a space before %: "400 %", "50 %". Degree sign directly after the number: "1°", "−10°".
  Multiplication sign as in the source: 2×, 3× ("2× upplösning").
- "dpi" and "ppi" lowercase, space before: "144 dpi", "300 ppi". "px" for pixels: "2 200 px".
- Dates: "18 september 2026" (day month year, month lowercase). ISO 2026-09-18 is also normal in Sweden.
- Times: "under en minut", "på 10 sekunder", "30+ minuter".
- Paper: A4 is the Swedish default; "US Letter" for Letter. Keep the English source's facts about Letter.
- Keyboard: Ctrl+F, Cmd+F (Mac), Ctrl+A / Cmd+A — as in the source.

### Keyword checks (qa_i18n.py) — how to pass them naturally

The checker counts a heading as containing the keyword when every word of `main_keyword` appears in it as a
substring. So inflected forms work only if they contain the base word:

- "få en pdf att se skannad ut": "får" contains "få" and "skannade" contains "skannad" → OK.
  Example H2: "Vad är det snabbaste sättet att få en PDF att se skannad ut?"
- "få ett dokument att se skannat ut": needs **skannat** (neuter) and **ett** in the heading.
  Example H2: "Hur får du ett dokument att se skannat ut?"
- "konvertera pdf till svartvitt": needs **svartvitt** (not "svartvit") and **konvertera** ("konverterar" OK).
  Example H2: "Hur konverterar du en PDF i färg till svartvitt?"
- "skanningseffekt": "skanningseffekten" contains it → OK.
- "pdf till skannad pdf" / "bild till skannad pdf": keep "till" and "skannad" in the heading.
- "skannad pdf": "skannade PDF:er" contains both words → OK.
